# Features

Every feature is tagged with the release that delivers it. The architecture and data model support all of them from the start.

Releases: **R1** Launch (web, PWA, Android) · **R2** Before you pay · **R3** Everyone · **R4** Merchants · **R5** Scale.

## 1. Discovery
- [R1] Home feed: trending, ending soon, new, picked by simple rules
- [R1] Categories: food, cinema and entertainment, fashion, groceries, electronics, travel and hotels, health and beauty, vehicles, education, services, online shopping
- [R1] Search with filters (category, discount size, card or bank, expiry, online or in-store), typo tolerant
- [R1] Merchant pages with all active offers, branches and hours
- [R1] Deal pages: terms, how to redeem, saving in LKR, end date, source link, last checked
- [R1] Nearby offers list by district or city (typed or chosen, no GPS needed)
- [R2] Map view of nearby offers
- [R2] Collections ("Weekend under 2,000", "Student deals") and festival hubs (Avurudu, Vesak, Christmas, Poya)

## 2. Save and alert
- [R1] Save a deal, with a reminder before it expires
- [R1] Follow a merchant, brand, category or keyword and get notified of new matching deals
- [R1] In-app inbox, email and web push; push on Android
- [R1] Controls: quiet hours, daily cap, daily or weekly digest
- [R2] Wishlist with target-price alerts
- [R3] WhatsApp alerts

## 3. My Wallet
- [R1] Pick banks, cards, telco and loyalty programs
- [R1] "Only deals I can use" filter
- [R1] Best card for this merchant, today
- [R1] Valid days shown on every offer, with "next valid date" and a "Remind me" button
- [R1] Card promotion expiry reminders
- [R2] Card Optimizer (premium): compares all your cards across offers and recommends one per purchase

## 4. Before you pay
- [R1] Share-to-Saveroo: share a product or store link (Android share and PWA share target) and get that merchant's best offers for your cards
- [R1] Merchant search and "request this store" button
- [R2] Browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox): detects checkout pages, suggests coupons, compares prices
- [R2] Shopping Mode (Android): temporary location permission while shopping, nearby and category offers
- [R2] In-store product lookup: search or scan a barcode, see offers and the best card
- [R2] Quick-launch shortcut and home screen widget
- [R3] iOS app and Safari extension
- [R4] In-app shopping browser with checkout detection

## 5. Coupons and redemption
- [R1] Copy-code coupons and show-code coupons
- [R2] "I used this" confirmation and personal savings tracker
- [R4] QR coupons with a cashier verification page
- [R4] Saveroo-exclusive codes negotiated with merchants
- [R4] Receipt scanning as proof of redemption

## 6. Community
- [R2] Users post deals with photos
- [R2] "Worked" and "expired" buttons, votes, comments, reports
- [R2] Points, badges and reputation
- [R2] Moderator tools
- [R3] Deal requests ("Anyone know an offer for X?")

## 7. Price intelligence
- [R2] Price history and "good price or not" indicator
- [R2] Cross-store comparison for the same product
- [R2] Savings summary: total saved this month and this year, as a shareable card

## 8. Rewards and cashback
- [R3] Streaks, daily check-in, referral rewards, levels
- [R5] Cashback on tracked purchases, paid out as vouchers or mobile top-ups

## 9. Machine learning
- [R1] Log events from day one (views, searches, saves, follows, redemptions). Ranking uses simple rules.
- [R5] Personalized ranking and notification timing
- [R5] Search that understands Sinhala, Tamil, English and Singlish
- [R5] Fake and expired deal detection from reports
- [R5] Recommendations ("people who saved this also saved...") and sale predictions

## 10. Built for Sri Lanka
- [R1] LKR, Asia/Colombo time, Poya and festival awareness, nationwide districts
- [R1] Fast on weak connections, small downloads
- [R1] WhatsApp sharing of deals
- [R3] Sinhala and Tamil, with a language choice in onboarding
- [R3] Low-data mode

## 11. Trust
- [R1] Verified label versus community label, source link and last-checked time on every deal
- [R1] Automatic expiry, report button
- [R1] Plain-language explanation of how Saveroo earns money

## 12. Growth and brand
- [R1] Shareable deal pages with preview images; public SEO pages for merchants, categories and deals
- [R3] Referral program, campus ambassadors
- [R2] "Saveroo saved me LKR X" story cards
- [R2] Events and weekly deal of the week

## 13. Operations (owner's back office)
- [R1] Admin dashboard: create, edit, schedule, pause, expire deals
- [R1] Deal form: merchant, branches, terms, validity, valid days, eligible cards, source, proof, images
- [R1] Bulk CSV import, duplicate warnings, recheck reminders, audit log
- [R2] Moderation queue for community posts

## 14. Merchant tools
- [R4] Self-serve merchant portal to post and manage offers
- [R4] Analytics: views, saves, redemptions
- [R4] Featured placement, campaigns, sponsored alerts
- [R4] Billing: pay per redemption, per click, or per week
- [R4] Off-peak deals
- [R4] Free merchant page and referral to a developer partner for websites

## 15. Platform
- [R1] Web, PWA, Android, admin
- [R1] Accounts: email link and Google sign-in (phone OTP later, SMS costs money)
- [R1] Privacy consents, data export and deletion
- [R1] Analytics, error monitoring, backups
- [R2] Premium subscription billing

## 16. Later
- [R5] Bank and telco partnerships and sign-up funnels
- [R5] Brand-funded product rebates
- [R5] Aggregated insights for merchants and brands
- [R5] Paid micro-surveys
- [R5] Gift cards and vouchers, group deals, tourist mode, employee benefits
