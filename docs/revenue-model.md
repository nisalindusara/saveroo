# Revenue model

Principle: free for users, paid by merchants and partners. Money comes after the audience, never before it.

## Stages

### Stage 1: launch (R1-R2). Goal is audience, revenue is small
- **Ads:** designed into the web layout from R1 but switched on in R2 once there is traffic. Put them on public SEO pages (merchant and deal pages), not inside the Android app, where they undercut "we save you money". Check ad network requirements and approval then.
- **Affiliate links:** each deal has an optional `affiliate_url`. Use it wherever a store has a program that accepts Sri Lankan publishers.
- **Premium v1 (R2):** see rules below.

### Stage 2: audience exists (R4)
- **Featured deal spots:** merchants pay per week or per click.
- **Pay-per-redemption:** merchant pays when a Saveroo user uses an offer. Needs codes, QR scans or receipt proof.
- **Sponsored alerts:** merchant pays to reach users who followed or saved something related. Saveroo does the targeting; the merchant never sees who the users are.
- **Off-peak deals:** merchants discount slow hours (cinema, cafe, salon, restaurant) and pay a fee per visit or a share of the extra sale.
- **Pay-per-signup offers:** banks, telcos and fintech apps pay per new customer.
- **Merchant websites:** free Saveroo page for every merchant, then refer those who want a full site to a vetted developer partner for a referral fee, with commission terms agreed in writing first.

### Stage 3: scale (R5)
- **Bank and telco partnerships:** biggest opportunity. Paid campaigns, sign-up referral fees, co-branded offers.
- **Brand-funded rebates:** packaged-goods brands pay for "buy X, get Rs. Y back" offers, proved by receipt scan.
- **Cashback:** share part of affiliate commission, paid out as vouchers or mobile top-ups. Do not hold user money or earn interest on balances without checking Central Bank of Sri Lanka rules.
- **Aggregated insights:** anonymous trends only ("3,000 Colombo users saved laptops this month").
- **Micro-surveys** and licensing the deal engine to banks or large retailers.

## Premium rules
The paid tier must obviously pay for itself.
1. The free tier stays very good. It is the growth engine.
2. Charge only for things that save measurable money: Card Optimizer, more and earlier alerts, price history, higher cashback rate, ad-free, later exclusive coupons.
3. Show a running counter ("You saved LKR X; premium costs LKR Y").
4. Offer a guarantee: refund or free extension if savings do not exceed the fee in the first three months.
5. Price well below We Love Deals (LKR 800 per month, LKR 7,000 per year as seen in Nov 2026) and test. One missed LKR 1,000 offer should pay for several months.
6. Do not sell premium exclusives until there are real exclusive deals. Launch it with Card Optimizer and alerts.

## Data policy
- Never sell individual user data. Never sell or share location.
- Sell only aggregated, anonymous insights and targeting as a service (the merchant pays for a campaign; no personal data leaves Saveroo).
- Collect only what a feature needs, with consent recorded in `consents`.
- The owner handles legal review of the privacy policy and terms before any public launch.

## Early warning list
- Vendors give deeper discounts when users have paid for membership. A free model needs volume before merchants take it seriously, so early discounts may be shallower.
- Stale or fake deals destroy an aggregator. Verification matters more than features.
- Do not scrape bank or merchant sites without checking their terms. Prefer partnerships.
