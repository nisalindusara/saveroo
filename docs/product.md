# Product

## One line
Saveroo is a free app that tells Sri Lankans the best deal, the best bank card and the best day to use it, before they pay.

## Why it exists
- The owner paid full price at the cinema, then learned an offer would have saved about LKR 1,000.
- At a shop counter with family, they found a bank card offer that applied on a different day. Coming back that day would have saved a lot.

Pattern: money is lost because the information arrives after the payment. Saveroo's job is to put the right offer in front of the person before they pay.

## Goal
Be the first name every Sri Lankan thinks of when they are about to spend money. Build the full vision from day one, deliver it in polished slices (see `features.md`), and give the first user the best version possible, not a rough one.

## Brand
- Name: Saveroo.
- Domain: saveroo.com is available; buy it once there is revenue. Until then use the free Cloudflare address.
- Social: Facebook handle owned. Instagram: the plain handle is taken by a small inactive account; use a variant such as saverooLK.
- Tone: friendly, direct, speaks in rupees ("Save LKR 1,000"), no hype.

## Users
- Everyday shoppers across Sri Lanka, in person and online.
- Bank card holders who do not know which offers apply to them.
- Students, families and young professionals who want to spend less.

## Principles
1. **Free for users.** Money comes from merchants and partners (see `revenue-model.md`).
2. **Fast.** Smooth on cheap Android phones and weak connections.
3. **Trusted.** Every deal shows its source and when it was last checked. Expired deals leave quickly.
4. **Sri Lanka first.** Local banks, telcos, Poya days, festivals, LKR, local payments, later Sinhala and Tamil.
5. **Private.** Minimum permissions. Never sell individual data. No raw location history.
6. **Big from day one.** The architecture covers the whole vision so nothing is rebuilt later.

## Launch supply
Offers are posted manually by the owner at first. Merchants are approached once there is an audience. Every offer needs a source link, validity dates, terms and eligible cards.

## Competition
- **We Love Deals** (welovedeals.lk): paid membership. As seen in Nov 2026: one-month free trial, then LKR 800 per month or LKR 7,000 per year, with about 150 vendors. Check the site again before using these figures in public.
- Other local platforms, bank and telco apps, and Facebook deal groups exist. Survey them before launch.

How Saveroo differs:
- Free for users.
- Offers unified by the user's own cards and telco, so they only see what they can use.
- "Before you pay" tools: valid-day reminders, share-to-Saveroo, browser extension, Shopping Mode.
- Community verification of deals.
- Public web pages for every merchant and deal, found through Google.
